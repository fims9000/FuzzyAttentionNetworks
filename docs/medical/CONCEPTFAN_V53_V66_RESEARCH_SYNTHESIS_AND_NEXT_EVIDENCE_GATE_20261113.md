# Research synthesis for ConceptFAN V53–V66: functional fidelity, predictive completeness, semantic identity

**Internal research synthesis, 13 November 2026. Not the camera-ready IOI/PIERE manuscript.** Primary conclusion follows **actual, retained** official GitHub Actions evidence and separates executed results from hypotheses. Objective: determine what can be honestly claimed about automatically generated temporal fuzzy concepts without oracle concept labels or physical source maps.

## Three distinct properties and non-implications

1. **Functional faithfulness:** given bounded fuzzy concept summaries `c_k`, a head `logit=b+sum_k beta_k(c_k-.5)` offers mathematically exact signed decomposition of that model's own decision. V56–V65 use it and verify no raw-to-risk shortcut. This says nothing about completeness relative to all information in `X`.
2. **Task-predictive completeness:** concept-only model must preserve sufficient information for the target, relative to a well-trained raw-input control with the same TRAIN label budget and a genuinely held-out partition. On UCI360, UCI309 and UCI322, simple raw logistic/Ridge controls often substantially outperform concepts, disproving broad predictive sufficiency for the current design.
3. **Semantic / physical source identifiability:** observed concept coordinates must correspond to distinct externally defined causes, not merely correlate with the outcome or with a correlated independent instrument. V66 shows a formal continuum of bounded concept coordinate reparameterizations giving the *exact same* predictions and different per-coordinate signed attributions. The mathematical construction does not negate identifiability under stronger a-priori assumptions that exclude mixing, but an exact-additive prediction is never itself a semantic certificate.

## Contributions defensible for a new research article

**Engineering contribution:** reproducible full sensor-to-concept-to-additive-head pipeline; unlabeled automatic source vocabulary and self-supervised temporal fuzzy memberships; independent source-link and fault consistency diagnostics under explicit physical reference assumptions; tested negative/failure scenarios and TRAIN-only provenance receipts. The reference-matching and sensor-fusion pieces are established methods; claim integrated testable system, not universal novelty.

**Empirical finding:** in different real sensor regimes, automatic statistical factor count and concept dimensionality are not reliable proxies for independently controlled physical source identities. A strong exact-attribution architecture can discard physically measurable concentration information. Preserving absolute state and recent time windows materially improves task utility on the SAME previously viewed physical TEST, but does not beat strong raw baselines or establish semantics.

**Theoretical finding:** for any K>=2, additive fuzzy risk scores remain invariant to doubly-stochastic invertible membership mixing with inverse-transformed coefficients, while individual concept meanings and signed contributions are not fixed. Therefore exact additive explanation does not mathematically prove a unique concept ontology. Additional assumptions and external measurement anchors are necessary.

## Publication-safe structure for IOI-related continuation

1. **Problem statement:** functional completeness vs task sufficiency vs semantic identifiability, define each separately.
2. **Model:** `X, M -> unsupervised source mapping -> static/native bounded fuzzy coordinates -> exact additive task head`. Explicitly forbid label gradients into source learning, distinguish 3 anonymous ICA statistical directions from 18 fuzzy membership states.
3. **Identifiability proposition:** present formal V66 mixing construction; state limitation that a restricted independent-source family may not be closed under mixing.
4. **Experiments:** V60 urban certified analyzers (negative source ambiguity), V61 independent gas flow manipulations (wrong K), V63 new independent dynamic dataset (loss), V64/V65 **posthoc exploratory** diagnosis and partial repair, V59 real clinical **abstention** for reference credibility. Include negative raw-model superiority and source/reference fault cases.
5. **Limitations:** only two real continuous sessions in UCI322, pre-observed TEST reused in V64/65, gas setpoints are not independent spectral chemical assays, no prospective clinical validation, insufficient theory to claim chemically unique factors.
6. **Conclusion:** improved testable inference and explicit conditions for trust, not automatic discovery of real clinical/chemical concepts.

## Acceptance criteria for a genuinely independent confirmatory study

Require **independent newly collected or genuinely untouched** recordings before selecting K/architecture/thresholds; at least three independent physical source interventions with known which source changed and controls where other sources remain fixed; device provenance and separate calibration/audited reference measurement; a second sensor apparatus or location; exact locked physical run-level TRAIN/VALID/TEST/embargo before reading outcome measurements; multi-run permutation and block-bootstrap uncertainty, no pseudo-replication; per-source independent conditional association, cross-talk specificity, expected reaction to controlled source interruption and no-source negatives; raw and concept-only equal-label, equal-target-budget comparisons; predeclared abstention when anchors missing; report ALL failures and CIs. Do not conflate a high Pearson with physical cause and do not use test-informed model selection to manufacture a positive result.

**Real hard blocker:** The currently available UCI/PhysioNet records do not provide multiple independent calibrated instruments + recorded fault labels + unobserved separate physical test sessions at the level needed to certify semantic source identity. More iterations against UCI322 with TEST already seen cannot meet that missing empirical criterion. Leave all research PRs draft; accepted IOI/PIERE files and main unchanged.
