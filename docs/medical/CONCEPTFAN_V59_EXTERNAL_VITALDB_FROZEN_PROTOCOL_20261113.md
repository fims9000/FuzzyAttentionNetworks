## Scientific boundaries and falsification
- **External holdout** is a new hospital dataset untouched by source tuning. Yet the model's input channel `RESP` changes source from impedance to capnography, patient physiology shifts from ICU to surgery, and PULSE/HR device algorithms/clock delays can differ. This is a joint domain shift; don't attribute changes causally to FAN architecture alone.
- **No new concept found on BIDMC remains a negative result**. VitalDB must not be used to select new V54 thresholds or supervised new source definitions.
- Adult/child population, patient status and prevalence might differ; AUPRC may be unestimable if sampled external TEST has one class. Preserve that outcome and unchanged cohort selection. NLL and direct monitor error remain reportable.
- VitalDB monitor HR is a target and comparator, not an independent physical assay of the newly discovered concept; V56/V57 physical corroboration claims remain unsupported. Case-level source site transfer does not prove causal or physiological identity.
- This study contributes **prospective external-site falsification** of the existing FAN research stack, not a trained new model. Avoid prospective device-use, medical risk, clinical generalization, or claimed model superiority before independent replicated cohorts. Source code/model weights remain in the research branch with a draft PR.

Original 53 BIDMC train files, methods, thresholds, patient allocation, model seeds, stress conditions and TEST decision remain frozen. Track hash receipts are computed on the decoded timestamp/value pairs. No external numeric TEST value, outcome distribution or model score was observed before this transport repair. Scientific validity requires keeping the original cohort selection intact; do not relax quotas or inclusion thresholds after any outcome appears.
