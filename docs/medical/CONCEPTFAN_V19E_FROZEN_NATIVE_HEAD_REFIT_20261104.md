# V19e — frozen native decision-head refit, no additional post-hoc predictor

## Motivation and observed evidence
V19d demonstrated that **the same frozen native K3 concepts** from V18/V19 contain more predictive signal than their jointly trained native risk readout currently uses. A train-only logistic probe on K3 native concept summaries achieved mean heldout AUPRC **0.65096 vs 0.58782** in the positive source mode and **0.68565 vs 0.60435** in the negative mode. This probe is a diagnostic and not an acceptable substitute for ConceptFAN's native decision architecture.

## New train-time capability
Implement a separate **TRAIN-only head-refit phase** in the existing native ConceptFAN graph. After end-to-end concept-generation training, freeze concept operators, thresholds, temporal shape pooling, observation-process head, dynamics, future predictor and reconstruction. Extract *only* the same K3 native concept summary and existing mask-only process contribution, with the original training histories in eval mode. Optimize the real model's physical decision_raw_weight and physical decision_bias by unweighted binary cross-entropy plus a fixed L2 penalty 0.001 on the **effective** decision coefficients (no validation/early stopping). Use deterministic PyTorch L-BFGS with at most 120 iterations and strong Wolfe line search. Preserve any existing per-slot TRAIN-screened signed policy, if present. Reinsert optimized values into the **same native parameters**, not a new classifier, and continue to compute predictions via original exact additive contributions. Training stage may be explicitly enabled or skipped. Train-only samples are identified and validated, with no access to heldout labels or sealed test episodes.

The refit step cannot change the native concepts, their symbolic operators or patient measurement reconstruction. Its success would isolate a weak jointly optimized native risk layer; it would not magically certify physiology.

## Frozen full-denominator ablation
Exactly 3 outcome modes (positive source, negative source, label-null) x 3 model initialization seeds (11,23,37) x 2 native temporal readout variants (legacy K3, learned temporal K3) = **18 completed native fits and 18 train-only native refits**. Use the **same already inspected** synthetic V18 dataset family: 800 TRAIN, 200 validation, 80 sealed test. Refit from the same 6-epoch K2 pretrained / 6-epoch K3 continued native models and disjoint TRAIN source/risk screen from V19b and V19d. Positive/negative modes initialize the new risk coefficient using that TRAIN-only screened association; outcome-null is a *deliberate forced-fitted diagnostic*, not a deployable accepted risk concept.

Compare **pre-refit native head**, **post-refit native head**, and independent V19d train-only logistic probe (V19d already versioned), all on the same heldout validation cohort. Metrics: AUPRC/AUROC/Brier, training head objective before/after, maximum exact native decomposition error, and explicit equality of all concept trajectories/forecast/reconstruction/process contributions before vs after refit. Report all 18 seeds/arms/modes, including regressions or objective failures; never claim improvement based only on a chosen run.

## Interpretation
If the native head refit closes much of the gap to the diagnostic logistic probe, optimization and multi-objective competition (forecast/reconstruction vs risk) are a major source of the current underperformance. If it does not, bounds/regularization on the native beta transform or feature representation may be the bottleneck. With only 3 seeds and already repeatedly viewed synthetic validation, results are strictly exploratory; no p-values or clinical validity claims. A measured factor is not a medically meaningful mechanism. Accepted PIERE camera-ready unchanged.
