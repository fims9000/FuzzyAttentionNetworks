# V61 preregistration: independently controlled REAL gas plumes vs discovered ConceptFAN sources

Written 2026-11-13 **before reading any V61 numeric VALID/TEST outcomes**. We selected the independent dataset after V60 exposed a one-dimensional bottleneck and non-unique CO/NOx associations. V61 is a NEW source family and real physical intervention; model choice is therefore post-V60 exploratory, not an originally confirmatory study.

## Experimental source and independent physical references

J. Fonollosa, UCI [Gas sensor array exposed to turbulent gas mixtures, id=309](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/309/gas+sensor+array+exposed+to+turbulent+gas+mixtures), DOI 10.24432/C5JS5P (CC BY 4.0); 180 **independent whole recordings** from a wind tunnel with 8 chemoresistive sensors. Two distinct real gas sources are independently flow-controlled: ethylene plus either CO or methane, each at zero/low/medium/high flow, typically 15 configurations per secondary gas and six repeated measurements each. A GC-MS system supplied validated **mean concentration** levels; it did not provide a time-resolved trajectory for every 100 ms. The 300-second protocol is 0–60 s clean-air baseline, 60–240 s exposure and 240–300 s recovery. The files encode experiment conditions in filenames. These labels are physical experiment metadata, NOT input to discovered concepts.

The eight original gas-sensor responses are the only concept/risk inputs. No temperature, relative humidity, file name, experiment identity, gas-level label, GC-MS concentration estimate or flow-setpoint enters the source rank/unsupervised encoder. Gas labels enter downstream TRAIN risk heads after source freezing and held-out source-concept association audits only.

## Frozen unit of inference and data split

- Select exactly the 180 officially downsampled 100 ms recordings (not duplicated raw 20 ms versions). Verify one unique file per experiment ID, 30 distinct gas configurations, exactly six replicates per configuration. Refuse incomplete archive rather than silently select favorable runs.
- Process each recording as 300 sequential **5-second median** bins of actual sensor readings? NO: **60 five-second bins** covering [0,300). Use time values rather than row count to avoid drifting sample rates; missing/empty bins must fail rather than be interpolated. One full recording produces ONE independent episode x[60,8], no target labels inside the sensor tensor.
- Stratify *within each gas configuration* by six independent recordings: deterministic seed **20261701**, 4 TRAIN, 1 VALID and 1 TEST => 120/30/30 whole recordings. No segments from one recording cross any split. These are repeated configurations, **not held-out novel gas compositions or independent sites**. Do not infer statistical power from 60 time bins as 60 independent replicates.
- TRAIN-only per-sensor median/IQR of observed sensor readings. Full trajectories selected with no classifier outcomes; fixed output signs/axes, no manual initial K2. Report source K, bootstrap interval, source mask and distributional support. Very small n_TEST=30 must be emphasized.

## Frozen architecture (full-run observable rank; true run-level independence)

The V53 clique proposal and V56 end-to-end source router require n>=300 episodes inherited from 16-step synthetic benchmarks, but there are only 120 genuinely independent TRAIN recordings. Replicating a recording into windows for proposal-fold support would **misrepresent independence**. Therefore V61 does NOT claim three-fold independent *group* proposal. Instead it uses the **existing TRAIN-only positive-lag observable spectral rank estimator** directly on the 120 complete TRAIN recordings (at least 80 independent runs, not 300 synthetic pseudo episodes), with 60 shuffled-run null repetitions, 20 bootstrap runs, lag=(1,2,3), familywise alpha=.05. This is an explicitly **SPECTRAL_ONLY** instrument with autonomous K, not a new source-rank theorem.

- Fit a V54 ObservableSpectralDefinitions to complete TRAIN trajectories. A K=0 outcome must remain bias-only. Do not increase K based on TEST gas labels.
- Fix the observable source basis and fuzzy initial thresholds/widths from unlabeled TRAIN ONLY. Train the V56 native causal diagonal recurrent fuzzy concept encoder using **three nonoverlapping 20-step segments per TRAIN recording** (360 segments total). These are correlated data augmentations for self-supervised optimization **only**, not independent source-estimation subjects or evaluation cases. 8 epochs, neural seed11, 20% random observed-cell hiding, denoise/forecast source objective. Freeze the network and source hash before any gas-label exposure.
- Train V55 static observable spectral fuzzy head and V56 native concept head on the identical 120 **WHOLE-RUN** TRAIN labels. Risk logit is an exact bias+SUM signed concept contribution, no raw bypass. Comparator raw time-summary LogisticRegression(C=1), PCA(K)+LogisticRegression, HistGradientBoosting(max_iter100,max_leaf_nodes15,l2=1), all with fixed parameters and identical run-level TRAIN targets. No TEST tuning.
- Three predeclared binary tasks: `ethylene_present` (ethylene flow > zero); `co_present` (secondary gas is CO and its flow > zero); `methane_present` (secondary is Methane and flow > zero). Zero means physical no delivery. These conditions are independently set by the apparatus. All three metrics are reported on all 30 VALID and all 30 TEST runs, no test-based thresholding or selection.
- As a separate semantic audit, compare per-run **exposure-minus-baseline** concept means with published GC-MS-validated nominal concentration levels for ethylene, CO and methane. Report complete K-by-3 Spearman matrix on VALID and TEST, raw eight-sensor comparator correlations and shuffled-condition negative control. These are **run-level** setpoints, not observed high-frequency gas concentrations. Report source sensitivity/sign and indistinguishability due to shared mixtures; do not name any axis as physical without independent specificity evidence.

## Failure and interpretation gates

- A discovered axis may track general gas exposure rather than one chemical. Correlation with ethylene, CO and methane can coexist; exact rank cannot prove gas identity. The 180 runs use one testbed and repeated conditions; do not generalize to patients, deployed urban sensors or unknown hardware.
- Actual interventions concern *gas delivery*, not calibrated independent sensor-device failures; this data does NOT verify V58 sensor-fault repair or independent instrument fault domains.
- A good supervised CO/Methane predictor might rely on correlated gas-array responses, not physically isolated concepts. Raw baselines may win and must be reported. A one-source K is not a property of physical gas mechanisms.
- TRAIN augmentation windows are explicitly dependent; never run patient/episode bootstrap over them for inferential confidence. Use whole-run bootstrap if discussing uncertainty.
- The conditional GC-MS levels validate mean exposures, NOT continuous 100ms ground truth, and the experiment filenames can encode metadata but are NEVER predictors or concept targets.
- Any parser/schema error is a blocker to be fixed without changing the frozen 4/1/1 split, thresholds, labels or gas source meaning.
- Nothing in this study edits accepted PIERE, IOI or earlier paper conclusions. Keep draft until independent replication and scientific peer review.
