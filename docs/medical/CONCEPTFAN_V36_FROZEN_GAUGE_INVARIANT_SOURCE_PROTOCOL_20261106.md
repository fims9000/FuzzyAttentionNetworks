## Existing priority
Classical second-order blind identification (AMUSE/SOBI; Belouchrani et al. 1997; review Pan et al. 2022, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9540980/), V14 multi-environment variance contrasts and V15 two-view, lagged covariance methods in this repository ALREADY find directions up to sign/permutation/scale under strong assumptions. ICLR 2024 Multi-view Causal Representation Learning with Partial Observability (Yao et al.) also establishes theoretical representations from shared views. **We do not claim source separation, gauge invariance or concept discovery as a new theorem.** The new capability here is a finite-sample *source-energy concept* tied to spectral ordering, mask-specific evidence and an explicit observational refusal policy, integrated with the native Temporal ConceptFAN risk head.

## Mathematical target: NOT a signed physical coordinate
At each t, two independent instrument views observe:
X_A,t = A S_t + eps_A,t; X_B,t = B S_t + eps_B,t,
with two uncorrelated weakly stationary sources, independent view noise and no cross-view measurement-noise covariance even across lags. A and B are unknown full-rank 2x2, estimated ONLY from anonymous TRAIN sensor histories/masks. Source 0 is defined observationally as **the mode with the largest positive lag-one autocorrelation**. Source 1 has a distinct (possibly negative) lag ratio. No human or outcome-based alignment to latent ground truth is used.

C_AB(0) = A D B.T, C_AB(1) = A diag(rho) D B.T; hence C1 C0^{-1} = A diag(rho) A^{-1}. Under full rank, simple separated eigenvalues and stable TRAIN bootstrap, eigenvectors identify A only up to column sign and scale and a permutation fixed by spectral ordering. Recover Bhat.T = Ahat^{-1} C0; Ahat/Bhat have inverse gauge. If uA = Ahat^{-1} XA and uB = Bhat^{-1} XB, the **cross-view product** uA,0 * uB,0 has expectation proportional to the slow mode's power and is invariant to arbitrary nonzero rescaling/sign of the recovered column when the two views are consistently calibrated by C0. It is ALSO exactly invariant to a simultaneous global device sign reversal XA→−XA, XB→−XB at inference with fixed TRAIN decomposition. It is NOT invariant to reversing only one view, and NOT proof of physical meaning or actual causal intervention.

Use only times when BOTH complete 2-sensor views are actually observed. Require at least 4 paired times in 16. Require a positive measured source cross-energy and dual independent views. When either view absent, when both views share too few timestamps, when the cross-energy contradicts TRAIN orientation, or when TRAIN eigenvalue separation/bootstrap fails, **refuse** the source and output EXACT native K2 prediction. An unseen one-view flip yields negative cross-energy in many cases; measure how often refusal actually protects, do not assert universal safety. Same-view/time colored noise is allowed if cross-view noise independence holds; *shared* colored noise is a falsification of that assumption.

Use only TRAIN measured episodes, no input matrix A/B, no known simulated sources S, no reference phantom/structural zeros to learn the decomposition. Independent sensor-view noise is external provenance ASSUMED by simulator; cannot be empirically guaranteed solely from the two observed views. No Type3 or Flow yet: their return is postponed until the concept is identified in the restricted observational sense.

## Stop / falsification
- If slow/fast rho equal or nearly tied: do NOT identify spectral index "slow".
- If independent view error provenance is absent: do NOT certify recovered axis.
- If view B missing: do NOT hallucinate posterior from Flow or Type-3.
- If both entire views inverted together: cross-product is invariant but inference of SIGNED physiological source still impossible.
- If common colored artifact is shared across views: noise-independence assumption violated and algorithm may yield a stable but nonphysical source. Report failure honestly.
- Even if prediction AUPRC is high, do not call it a new mathematical theorem. The goal is a narrow falsifiable capability showing what can be automatically *measured* and what remains impossible to *name*.
