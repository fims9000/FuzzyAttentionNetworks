## Problem and falsifiable hypothesis

V37's three cross-view covariance agreement can be driven by a shared, unobserved instrument artifact. Pure agreement cannot certify physical source identity, regardless of number of channels, higher-order fuzzy membership, or generative reconstruction. V38 adds TWO independent external evidential mechanisms:

1. **Exogenous controlled reference response**: a randomized, non-outcome-labeled diagnostic ACTUATOR excites one physical source in an independent instrument calibration batch, recorded as paired off/on six-channel trajectories. A **negative-control intervention** excites the other source at identical intensity. The measured V37 source feature should respond more to the named actuator than the irrelevant actuator. Simulator knows which source the actuator physically excites; source-learning algorithm sees only *paired measured observations and externally supplied actuator type*, **never the actual latent source vectors or mixing matrices**. This is substantial extra supervision; not unsupervised physical semantics. Even a positive response only certifies coupling to this instrument intervention under assumptions, not that a routinely observed clinical signal has a unique biological cause.

2. **Negative-control instrument channel**: source-independent, externally designated sensor 10 monitors acquisition artifact rather than physical sources. On normal TRAIN it reads low independent sensor noise. A shared time-correlated instrument artifact (visible as common bias on the six views) simultaneously enters this channel if — and ONLY if — it is wired to that artifact. On TEST require sufficient actually observed sentinel samples and a TRAIN-calibrated threshold on demeaned measured sentinel variance. If the artifact *bypasses* sentinel, observational ambiguity remains; include that as explicit negative control. **This is an external measurement and instrument design assumption, not discovery of a new independent sensor by ConceptFAN.**

Under healthy sensor view independence and trustworthy calibrations, the new concept can be interpreted narrowly as energy of the temporally slow measured mode responsive to specified actuator. No signed latent source coordinate, causality of clinical outcome, or real device quality certified.

## Stop conditions/negative evidence

- If intervention does not distinguish the target from nuisance under our frozen thresholds, **refuse** the V38 source and report the entire result including reduced coverage; do not recalibrate after TEST.
- Shared artifact not observed in sentinel **may remain undetected**, even though probe was successful; this is a fundamental external-monitoring limitation.
- If sentinel is unavailable, require K2 fallback regardless of V37 ABC evidence; this extra strictness can degrade useful source coverage.
- Probe pulses reaching instrument sentinel undermine interpretation; fail calibration instead of certifying a physiological concept.
- A positive intervention response to a controlled *simulator* source is not proof of physical biology or semantic interpretability in human datasets.
- If proof-of-concept is validated only on these two synthetic AR devices, do NOT claim new mathematical identifiability theorem, generalization to other domains, or superiority to published CBMs. Baseline novelty boundary remains: classical SOBI, negative controls, and instrumental variables are existing ideas; scientific claim is an *operational audit/refusal protocol*.
