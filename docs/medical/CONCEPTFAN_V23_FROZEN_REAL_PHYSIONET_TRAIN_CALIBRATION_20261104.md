# ConceptFAN V23 — FROZEN REAL PHYSIONET TRAIN-only calibration and matched external predictors (2026-11-04)

## Why a real experiment now
V22a–f on fully synthetic multivariate sources established that correctly trained native concepts can recover identifiable simulator factors, but cannot thereby certify physical identities. In V22e ConceptFAN's uncalibrated synthetic probabilities had Brier ~.235–.242 versus simple logistic ~.159–.176; V22f showed a **standard two-parameter positive affine transform of the native risk logit** can reduce Brier while preserving concept contributions, but not guarantee logistic-level calibration. Repeated variations of the same synthetic generators cannot resolve clinical validity.

V23 tests the *same calibration principle* on the **official real PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2012 Set A**, using frozen input/feature/split selection from the already executed V10 clinical baseline [GitHub Actions 41623853277] and its external model comparison [41626717843]. This is **not** a new source identifiability or clinical causality test and must not be marketed as physical concept discovery.

## Frozen 2400 TRAIN label budget
Within original TRAIN 2400, use one deterministic **stratified 2000 native fitting / 400 reserved TRAIN calibration** split (seed 20261104). The calibrator has no shared patient labels with the model gradients. Raw feature selection, train-only normalization and rank K estimation may use all 2400 TRAIN **measurements** but no heldout TRAIN calibration labels. This is a partially transductive unsupervised TRAIN measurement preprocessing design, not an external independent hospital split.

1. Native V10 multiscale + internal mask consistency only (no V7–V9 tuning arms), exactly frozen prior 18-epoch objective/configuration, batch 128, initialization seeds 11/23/37, K=8 physical native concepts plus independent observation-policy concept branch. No new V22 synthetic source-risk gates or clinical causal semantic labels. Original exact additive native logit and future/reconstruction tasks must remain intact.
2. Fit on 400 disjoint TRAIN calibration patient outcomes **only after** all 2000-patient native model weights are frozen. Standard two-parameter positive affine recalibration: `z_cal = shift + slope * z_native`, `slope ∈ [0.05,50]`, `shift ∈ [-6,6]`, deterministic L-BFGS-B NLL objective plus `0.002*(log(slope)^2+shift^2)`, initial 1/0, max 200 iterations. This is familiar **Platt scaling**, not new concept discovery. No native weights are updated.

Baseline logistic regression and HistGradientBoosting (same **existing** `external_baselines_v10.py`, same config `pd_v10_vs_external_20261103.yaml`) trained on original **all 2400 TRAIN labels**, the same selected 12 measured channels, 36h histories, observed masks and recorded clinician observation schedule. These are full labeled-budget predictive controls, not official concept bottleneck comparisons.
4. All models are evaluated exactly once, without fitting or choosing parameters from outcome labels of the previously inspected 300 development patients. Report per-seed raw vs calibrated native AUPRC, AUROC, Brier, log-loss and exact scaled physical/care additive logit error; baseline same metrics; all three original model seeds and no excluded negative cases.

## Mathematical faithfulness and go/no-go
If original risk logit is `z = b + Σ physical_k + Σ observation_policy_j`, the global positive calibration has decomposition
`shift+slope*b + Σ slope*physical_k + Σ slope*observation_policy_j`.
It cannot introduce a raw-sensor-to-prediction bypass or transform a measured statistical concept into a physiological diagnosis. Every original native model state digest must match its post-calibration and post-evaluation digest. AUPRC and AUROC must remain unchanged under positive affine probability calibration except machine precision; otherwise mark run FAILED. Brier/log-loss may improve or worsen — report both and do not tune the predeclared bounds.

## Scientific boundaries
The 300 development outcomes have already been used in repeated V10 studies and are **not** a valid independent confirmatory cohort. The native model sees 2000 gradient-training labels + 400 independent calibration labels; the external methods see all 2400 in their predictor optimization. Equal TOTAL labeled patient budget does not mean equal gradient/compute. Calibration observations were included without labels in original train-only source estimation. No independent hospital or hidden physical states/doctor labels available. Actual physical source identity, causal attribution and clinical safety must not be claimed from successful AUPRC/Brier changes.
