## Scientific question and priority boundary
V36 can recover an unsigned, time-ranked factor from TWO instrument views without knowing mixing matrix H, but (1) used uncentered lag moments while calling them covariance, (2) evaluated only one fixed H, (3) was damaged by unmodelled common colored measurement noise appearing in both views, and (4) lacked a preplanned one-view offset control. Cross-view latent separation by lag moments is a classical idea (AMUSE/SOBI, Belouchrani et al. 1997); adding a third view/cross-check is NOT a new identifiability theorem. Our narrow contribution is measurable **evidence provenance and selective admission** into frozen Temporal ConceptFAN under clearly stated, falsifiable assumptions.

V37 has THREE independently noisy *observational views*, predetermined sensor groupings A=(4,5), B=(6,7), C=(8,9). The 2×2 mixing matrices A/B/C are unknown to the learner and separately randomly sampled for each independent replicate. Group assignment and cross-view noise independence **remain simulator-given external assumptions**, not automatically recovered causal structure.

Each independently discovered pair AB, AC, BC has the same lag-order target (largest real lag1 spectral eigenvalue), own 2×2 blind decomposition and power \`E_pair\`. Center measured values separately for every TEST/TRAIN episode and pair on its actual jointly observed timestamps BEFORE computing moments and source energy. This removes **constant per-episode sensor offsets** exactly, but destroys the original episode-mean source magnitude: the identifiable target here is the **energy of fluctuations around that episode's measured baseline**, NOT absolute energy, NOT a signed physical factor. Compute C_AB(0) and C_AB(1) from these demeaned observations, not raw uncentered second moments. All covariance bias and finite-16-step identification caveats remain.

Point feature for V37 = median of three pairwise TRAIN-normalized source memberships q_AB/q_AC/q_BC, only when **all THREE independent pairs** individually have >=4 fully paired observed times and strictly positive cross-power >=0.10 (in raw projection units). Refuse if a pair is invalid, a view completely absent, or max pairwise discrepancy in membership exceeds an **unsupervised TRAIN-only threshold**: max(0.12,95th percentile TRAIN max pairwise discrepancy among all three-supported episodes); no threshold fitted from outcome labels. Never use hidden true S or A/B/C for fitting, ordering, threshold selection or VALID gate; only train inputs X+mask. Pair AB alone acts as the centered two-view competitor. Old V36 uncentered, ordinary raw temporal logistic and quadratic cross-view logistic with ALL available labels act as explicit comparators. A separate exact arithmetic source-only comparison verifies baseline-offset invariance. The original Temporal ConceptFAN K2 remains frozen, new γ fitted TRAIN labels only; dual independent VALID400 admissions per world, signed gamma used only when both env outcomes have significant NLL gain. Regardless of source, no full-support → EXACT old K2 logit, not a hallucinated posterior.
